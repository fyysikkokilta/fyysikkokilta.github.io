## Description

### TLDR
Introduction to Topology, could be taken with no previous knowledge.

### General

General Topology is a rigorous introduction to the theory of general topological spaces. The course develops the fundamental notions of topology, continuity, convergence, compactness, connectedness, and separation, and proceeds to more advanced topics such as metrization, quotient spaces, homotopies, topological vector spaces, and weak topologies.

Topology is a fundamental branch of mathematics with applications throughout mathematics and other disciplines. The course begins with the basic building blocks of topological spaces and develops the theory through proofs and exercises.

### Learning outcomes (official)
* developing the logical reasoning skills to write rigorous proofs in mathematics
* ability to describe fundamental topological concepts such as continuity, convergence, and compactness
* applying basic definitions and results in topology to evaluate the properties of various topological spaces
* building a solid foundation in set theory and its operations

### Content (official)
* Topological spaces
* Continuity and convergence
* Product topology
* Compactness and Connectedness
* Separation and countability axioms
* Urysohn's lemma and metrization theorem
* Topological vector spaces

## Practicalities

The course has two main weekly sessions.

The sessions primarily consist of solving exercises and proving results related to the week's topics. Students can receive assistance from the teacher and assistant during the sessions.

Students are expected to study the assigned reading and/or video material **before** each session.

Exercise credits form the majority of the course grade. Students who cannot attend the weekly sessions can alternatively obtain the corresponding credits by submitting written solutions.

There are also 2–3 homework exercises per week.

### Assessment

The course grade consists of:

| Component | Weight |
|-----------|--------|
| Exercises | 60% |
| Final exam | 40% |

The weekly exercises consist of:

- participation in the exercise sessions;
- alternatively, submitting written solutions to the session exercises;
- weekly homework assignments.

The final exam is a smaller component than the exercises, but a minimum exam score is required to pass the course. The exam was no more difficult than the course exercises.

The minimum requirements were:

| Grade | Required total score |
