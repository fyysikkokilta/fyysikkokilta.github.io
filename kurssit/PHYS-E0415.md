## Description

The course continues {% include k.html id="PHYS-C0220" %} Thermodynamics and Statistical Physics. The emphasis is on applying statistical mechanics to solve a wide variety of problems, ranging from phase transitions in condensed matter to applications outside traditional physics.

## Course material

### Official material

The official textbook is *Statistical Mechanics: Entropy, Order Parameters and Complexity* by James P. Sethna. Weekly lecture slides are uploaded to MyCourses, but the textbook is the primary learning resource.

### Extra material

Lecture notes from other universities can be useful for individual topics.

## Contents and workload

### Overall workload

{% include stars.html count=2 %}

Although the amount of material is large, much of it is supplementary. The workload depends on the computational project and on whether you choose to take the optional exam.

| Week | Topics |
|---|---|
|1|Basics|

|5|Percolation|
|6|Phase transitions, stability, metastability|

|9|Relative entropy, Jarzynski equality|

The presentation and computational project are completed in groups. Weekly exercise sessions provide support.
