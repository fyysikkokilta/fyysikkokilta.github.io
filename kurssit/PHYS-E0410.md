## Description

This course continues the first quantum mechanics course and expands the foundations needed for advanced quantum mechanics topics.

## Course material

### Official material

No official textbook. Recommended references include Griffiths, Cohen-Tannoudji, Ballentine, and Schwabl. Excellent lecture notes and model solutions are provided.

### Extra material

A YouTube playlist with videos related to the course.
