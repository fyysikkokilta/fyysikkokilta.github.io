## Description

Compulsory course introducing fundamental data structures and algorithms in Scala. Topics include sorting, searching, hashing, dynamic programming, graph algorithms, and parallel algorithms.

## Course material

### Official material

A+ online platform with weekly chapters, quizzes, programming exercises, and an optional textbook based on *Introduction to Algorithms*.

### Extra material

IntelliJ IDEA documentation and online videos.

## Contents and workload

### Overall workload

{% include stars.html count=3 %}

Approximately 7–9 hours of work per week. The middle weeks are the most demanding.

### Weekly contents

| Week | Topics |
|---|---|
|1|Big-O notation, basic data structures|
|2|Sorting algorithms|
|3|Trees and heaps|
|4|Binary search trees|
|5|Hashing|
|6|Dynamic programming|
|8–9|Graphs and graph algorithms|
|10|Parallel algorithms|
|11|NP-completeness, graph colouring|
|12|Extra assignments|
|13–14|Revision and final exam|

## Practicalities

Weekly A+ programming exercises together with a mandatory final written exam. Exercise sessions are available.
