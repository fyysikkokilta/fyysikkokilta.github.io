## Description

The course could be described as Calculus 4, as it partly continues discussing similar topics expanding them on the complex plane. The course is well-organized and nice to follow. Upon successful completion of this course, you should be able to make use of complex numbers in solving geometric problems, be able to solve mapping problems in the plane, recognize the behavior of complex functions, be able to interpret basic properties of analytic (holomorphic and meromorphic) functions and be able to calculate real integrals by making use of complex integrals.
