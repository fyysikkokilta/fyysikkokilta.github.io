## Minor 20-25 cr

TFM students complete a 20-25 cr minor. The minor and elective studies together make up 40 cr. The minor is usually chosen quite freely from Aalto or another university, but it cannot be a minor derived from the student’s own major.

## Elective Studies 15-20 cr

Electives may include university-level courses, a second minor, exchange studies, or 1-10 cr of practical training. They must not overlap with other courses in the degree.
